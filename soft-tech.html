Philosophy

Real hardware, three quiet rules.

Every sensor, chip, and material we choose is measured against the same three ideas.

Quiet

No push notifications. Devices signal through light and gentle motion, processed entirely on-device.

Warm

Custom silicon, wrapped in soft materials and rounded resistance instead of cold glass and hard clicks.

Present

Each processor runs one task well, then drops to microwatt idle — nothing to configure daily.

How it works

Sense, process, respond — entirely on-device.

No cloud round-trip. The soft-core reads its environment and reacts locally, in under 15 milliseconds.

01 · sense

Ambient sensors

Light, sound, motion, and proximity sampled continuously at microwatt power draw.

02 · process

Soft-core chip

A dedicated low-power processor interprets patterns on-device — nothing leaves the room.

03 · respond

Ambient output

Light shifts, sound softens, or nothing happens at all — the correct response, most of the time.

Queries

Questions, answered plainly.

The things people ask us most, plus a direct line if yours isn't here.

Q01Does any data leave the device?
No. Sensing and inference both run on the soft-core chip inside the device itself. Nothing is sent to a server unless you explicitly enable the optional companion app.
Q02What happens if power is lost?
Devices resume their last state automatically. There's no cloud sync to wait on, so a Soft Tech device is usable again the instant power returns.
Q03Can I self-host or inspect the firmware?
Yes. Firmware source and the on-device inference pipeline are published on our developer portal for anyone who wants to audit or extend it.
Q04How long is the sensor response time?
Typically 12 milliseconds from sensor read to output, since inference happens locally on the soft-core chip with no network round-trip.
Q05Do I need an app to use a Soft Tech device?
No app is required for any device to function. A companion app exists only for optional configuration and is never required day to day.
